Grain and oilseed trade

A key part of our business

Grain trade is a key part of our company's business. Our purchase points are conveniently located next to the land of the farmers we work with, and the grain we buy is stored in our own storage facilities and silos.

Every grain purchase goes through all quality controls and checks so that we maintain a high level of food safety, as confirmed by our ISCC EU, ISO 9001:2015, HACCP and GMP+ B3 certification.
