About Cezareja

A private Croatian company since 1997

Cezareja d.o.o. was founded on 9 September 1997. Our core business is the wholesale of grain, mineral fertilisers, seed and crop protection products.

We build our partnerships on honesty, quality and an individual approach, developing good long-term relationships.
